# Mission: STAT 251 Midterm (UBC)

## Why
Score well on the STAT 251 midterm, which is worth about 22% of the grade, on Friday Oct 30, 2026 (8:00–8:50am, 50 minutes). There is no make-up exam.

## Success looks like
- Can do every question type in the instructor's 3 practice sets from a blank page, without looking at solutions.
- Shows the steps that earn partial marks: defines the RV, states the distribution with its parameters, writes the formula, then substitutes.
- Uses the provided z-table quickly in both directions (z → probability, and probability → z).
- Arrives with a one-sided handwritten cheat sheet that focuses on personal weak spots.

## Constraints
- 7 days, from Oct 23 to Oct 30.
- Exam format: multiple choice, short answer, and short computation. Calculator only, no R.
- Coverage (course notes): Ch 1 descriptive; Ch 3 probability; Ch 4 random variables (density/F, quantiles, E/Var, sums & averages, max/min); Ch 5 normal; Ch 6 Bernoulli/binomial, geometric & return period, Poisson process (+ exponential), Poisson approximation to the binomial. Up to the Oct 28 lecture.

## Out of scope
- R coding.
- Ch 2 (regression), Ch 7 (CLT; but normal approximation to the binomial §7.2 appears in practice set 3, so treat it as maybe), Ch 8+.
