# STAT 251 Midterm Resources

## Knowledge

- Instructor's Midterm Practice Problems 1–3 with solutions (local PDFs in workspace root)
  The highest-trust source: they show the question style, the marking scheme, and the notation, e.g. N(μ, σ²) with the variance as the 2nd parameter. Use for: modelling every drill question, and deciding which steps earn partial marks.
- Course notes: `textBook_coursenotes.pdf` (179 pp; PDF page = book page, so link with #page=N)
  The official text. Notation: density f(x), distribution function F(x), Q(α), Med(X), N(μ, σ²). Key pages: §4.3–4.4 p.63–74, §4.5 p.74, §4.6 p.77, Ch 4 exercises p.82, Ch 5 p.89, Ch 6 p.103 (geometric p.106, Poisson p.108, Poisson approx p.113).
- Exercise solutions: `Chapter3Solutions.pdf`, `Chapter4-Question23.pdf` (max of uniforms), `Chapter5Solutions.pdf`, `Chapter6Solutions.pdf`
  Use for: extra worked problems in the course's own style.
- [Course outline, STAT 251 W1 2024 (UBC Statistics)](https://r9e-web.stat.ubc.ca/sites/default/files/course_outlines/syllabus_stat_251_w1_2024.pdf)
  Maps chapters to topics: Ch 1 descriptive, Ch 3 probability, Ch 4–6 random variables and distributions, Ch 7 CLT. Use for: scoping what counts as midterm material.
- Book: *Probability and Statistics for Engineering and the Sciences*, J.L. Devore (listed as an additional reference in the outline)
  Several practice problems come from Devore (kangaroo-rat exponential, diode voltages). Use for: extra exercises with the same flavour.
- [Penn State STAT 414: Introduction to Probability Theory](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat414/)
  Free, well-maintained university notes (CC BY-NC). Use for: second explanations of probability rules and of discrete and continuous distributions.
